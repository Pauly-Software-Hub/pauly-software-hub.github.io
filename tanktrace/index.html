TankTrace

Stops fuel theft at the depot - dips, deliveries and every litre accounted for

TankTrace keeps the fuel of a depot or filling station under control. Attendants send the tank dips, loading and pump photos from their phones; TankTrace reads them, works out what should be in every tank and shows straight away where litres go missing. The number plate of a vehicle picks the client, so fuel is never given on the wrong account.

What it does

  • Tank dips and deliveries read from phone photos
  • Shows the difference between what should be in the tank and what is there
  • Number plate check: the plate picks the client and account
  • Pump meter readings before and after every fill
  • Alerts on WhatsApp or Linkora
  • Reports and Excel exports for the office

Good to know

  • Updates download and install by themselves.
