ModSmith Studio

Make, check, merge and clean game mods for DayZ, ARK, Satisfactory, FiveM and Palworld

ModSmith Studio is a mod workshop for DayZ, ARK: Survival Ascended, Satisfactory, FiveM (GTA V) and Palworld. It checks mods for problems and suspicious code such as backdoors, merges many mods into one, and helps you create new mods from photos and text.

What it does

  • Checks mods for problems and backdoors
  • Removes suspicious code
  • Merges many mods into one mod
  • Creates mods from photos and text
  • DayZ, ARK Ascended, Satisfactory, FiveM and Palworld

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 14 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
