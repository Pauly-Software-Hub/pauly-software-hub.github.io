FleetKeeper

Licence discs, roadworthies, services and fuel for every vehicle

FleetKeeper keeps track of every vehicle and driver in a fleet: licence disc and roadworthy expiry dates, services, driver's licences and PrDPs, and fuel cost per kilometre. It reminds you on WhatsApp before anything runs out, so no vehicle is stopped at a roadblock or misses a service.

What it does

  • Licence discs, roadworthies and services with reminders
  • Driver's licences and PrDP expiry dates
  • Fuel use and cost per km for each vehicle
  • WhatsApp reminders before anything expires
  • Works for bakkies, trucks, cars and bikes

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • Free demo mode, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
