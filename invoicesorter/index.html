Invoice Sorter

Invoices filed into the right client folder by themselves

Invoice Sorter reads tax invoices - PDFs, scans and photos sent on WhatsApp - finds the client on each one and files it into that client's folder. It keeps a list of every invoice it filed, so the office never searches for a lost invoice again.

What it does

  • Reads PDF invoices and photos of invoices
  • Finds the client on every invoice and files it in the right folder
  • Picks up invoices sent on WhatsApp
  • A list of every invoice filed, by client and date
  • Runs by itself in the background

Good to know
