Orbit Agent Studio

A team of AI agents that works on your projects with you

Orbit Agent Studio is a multi-agent AI workspace in one program. A team of AI agents plans, writes and checks work together in project folders, using free cloud AI first and a built-in local model as a backup, with no extra software to install. It is made to run smoothly on ordinary PCs.

What it does

  • Several AI agents that work together as a team
  • Free cloud AI first, local AI on your PC as a backup
  • Picks the right local model for your computer
  • Works in your project folders and can run the apps it makes (with your approval)
  • One program - nothing else to install

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 14 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
