CCTV Watchdog

Turns the cameras you already have into a guard that WhatsApps you

CCTV Watchdog connects to the IP cameras already on your network, finds them by itself, and watches for movement after hours or a camera that stops working. When something happens you get a WhatsApp alert with a picture, so a business or depot is watched without paying for a new camera system.

What it does

  • Finds the cameras on your network automatically
  • After-hours movement alerts with a picture on WhatsApp
  • Warns when a camera goes offline
  • Set the hours and areas to watch per camera
  • Runs quietly in the background next to the clock

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • Free demo mode, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
