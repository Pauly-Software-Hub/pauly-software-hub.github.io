Money Guardian

Find forgotten debit orders, check municipal bills and keep life admin in one place

Money Guardian looks after the household money admin: it finds debit orders and subscriptions in your bank statements, writes cancel and dispute letters, reads your municipal bills so mistakes stand out, and keeps important documents in a private vault.

What it does

  • Finds debit orders and subscriptions in your statements
  • Cancel and dispute letters written for you
  • Reads municipal bills from the PDF
  • Private vault for life-admin documents
  • Send photos from your phone to the PC

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • Free demo mode, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
