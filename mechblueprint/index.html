Mech Blueprint

A 3D exploded view of the vehicle - click a part to see it come out

Mech Blueprint shows a vehicle as a 3D blueprint for mechanics. Click a part such as the alternator and the bolts loosen and the part moves out, so you can see how it comes off before you start. Part numbers from different brands are shown with each part.

What it does

  • 3D exploded views of vehicle parts
  • Click a part to see the bolts loosen and the part come out
  • Part numbers from different brands
  • Cars, bakkies, trucks, bikes and tractors

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 14 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
