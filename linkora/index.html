Linkora

Business chat for your company - your staff and clients in one safe place

Linkora is a chat app made for a business. The company owns the accounts: staff and clients chat, call and share files, and when someone leaves you switch them off at once. Clients only see your staff, never each other. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, and any message can become a task with a due date. Runs on the PC, Android phones and iPhones.

What it does

  • Chats, groups and announcement channels
  • Voice and video calls, screen sharing from the PC
  • End-to-end encrypted - the company owns the accounts
  • Clients only see your staff, never other clients
  • Turn any message into a task with a due date
  • PC program, Android app and iPhone (web app)
  • One licence for the whole company - 30-day free trial, buy or renew inside Linkora

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 30 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
