SmartBasket

Scan your till slips and see where groceries are cheapest

SmartBasket reads your till slips from a photo and keeps the price history of everything you buy, so you can see which shop is cheapest, when prices go up, and how you are doing against your budget.

What it does

  • Scan till slips with a photo
  • Price history for every item
  • See which shop is cheapest
  • Shopping list and monthly budget

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • Free demo mode, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
