ImageGuard

Find where your photos are shared online and get them taken down

ImageGuard helps people whose photos were leaked, stolen or used for blackmail. It searches the internet for your images, shows where they are shared, and helps you send the right takedown requests to get them removed.

What it does

  • Searches the internet for your images
  • Shows every link where a photo was found
  • Helps you write and send takedown requests
  • Collects evidence and tracks every takedown request

Good to know
