ScamShield

Check a message, link or invoice before you trust it

ScamShield checks messages and links for the signs of a scam, so you can tell whether that SMS, WhatsApp or e-mail is safe before you click or pay. It also checks supplier invoices for changed bank details - a common way businesses are robbed - and keeps a list of scam numbers.

What it does

  • Checks messages for scam signs
  • Checks links before you open them
  • Checks invoices for changed bank details
  • Keeps a list of scam numbers
  • Copy a warning to send to family

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • Free demo mode, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
