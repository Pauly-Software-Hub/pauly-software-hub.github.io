Tarkov Map Companion

Interactive raid maps for Escape from Tarkov that open by themselves

Tarkov Map Companion opens the right interactive map when you load into a raid and shows where you are with an arrow that turns with you. Floors, layers and quest points can be switched on and off, with a choice of map styles.

What it does

  • Opens the right map when a raid loads
  • Your position as an arrow that turns with you
  • Floors and switchable map layers
  • Quest points kept up to date
  • Several map styles

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.

  • Updates download and install by themselves.
