Corvanta

Orders, invoices, statements and stock for distributors and wholesalers

Corvanta runs the office of a distribution business on one Windows computer: the product catalogue and price lists, clients with their own discounts and terms, orders, tax invoices, statements with 7/30/60/90-day ageing, stock and specials. Clients can order on their phones and the orders arrive straight in the program. Add-ons cover POS tills, accounting, payroll and delivery tracking.

What it does

  • Catalogue and price lists with per-client discounts and time-limited specials
  • Tax invoices, statements and age analysis, e-mailed to clients
  • Phone ordering app for your clients (Android and iPhone)
  • Reps, drivers and delivery routes with load and offload photos
  • Documents filed into client folders automatically
  • Add-ons: POS tills, accounting, payroll, delivery and tracking

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 30 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
