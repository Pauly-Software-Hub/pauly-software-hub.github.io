SlipSnap

Scan toll, fuel and grocery slips and pump totalisers with your phone

SlipSnap turns the phone camera into a slip scanner. It reads toll gate, fuel and grocery slips - place, date, total, VAT and litres - and reads fuel pump totalisers to show how much was pumped since the last reading. Everything works on the phone itself, also offline, and exports to Excel.

What it does

  • Reads toll, fuel and grocery slips: place, date, total, VAT, litres
  • Reads fuel pump totalisers and works out the difference
  • Takes the photo by itself when the slip is sharp
  • Works offline - no cost per scan
  • Totals per category and export to Excel

Good to know

  • For Windows 10 and Windows 11 – one program file, nothing else to install.
  • 14 days free, then a Monthly or Yearly subscription (EFT or card).
  • Updates download and install by themselves.
  • Made and supported in South Africa by Paul Maritz.
