PrintForge Studio

Everything for 3D printing in one program - press one button for the best settings

PrintForge Studio slices your models for Creality, Bambu Lab, Prusa, UltiMaker, Anycubic, Elegoo, Voron and other printers. One button finds your printer on USB or the network, studies the model and sets layer height, speeds, temperatures, infill and supports - supports only where needed, with a clean gap so they come off easily and leave a smooth surface. It also controls and watches your printer, lets you build your own models from shapes and text, and turns photos into print-ready 3D models.

What it does

  • Auto-detect best settings - every choice explained
  • Slicer with tree-like and snug supports that are easy to remove
  • Finds printers on USB and Wi-Fi: Creality K-series, Klipper, OctoPrint, Bambu Lab
  • Print, watch and control your printer from the PC
  • Make your own models: shapes, text, holes, smooth and hollow
  • Photo to 3D model: AI figurines, keychains, cookie cutters, lithophanes
